# Puzzle Generation

The long-term benchmark requires many independent scenarios with controlled solvability, difficulty, and solution-space properties.

## Subproblems

Puzzle generation consists of three coupled problems:

1. sampling formal languages,
2. characterizing their solution spaces,
3. constructing boards and racks with the desired properties.

Each evaluation instance should present a fresh board. A chain of internally generated states may be used to create it, but that chain is not part of the model context.

## Witness Principle

A simple way to guarantee solvability is to construct a board transition

\[
B_t \rightarrow B_{t+1}
\]

with a known legal move, then expose \(B_t\) and a rack containing the required new symbols. The known move acts as a witness.

## Overlap-Only V1

V1 requires every move after the first to overlap at least one existing tile. Adjacency without overlap is not sufficient.

## Criss-Cross Construction

In two or three dimensions, boards can be generated by repeatedly placing valid words through existing anchors along different axes. This produces connected structures and naturally creates cross-word constraints.

## Unbounded Coordinates

The logical board may use an unbounded sparse coordinate system. For presentation and analysis, the occupied region can be translated into a finite bounding box or region of interest.

## Generation Budget

Generation must terminate predictably. One possible budget stops construction after eight consecutively sampled words cannot be placed. More advanced versions may distinguish failures caused by language sampling, rack constraints, and spatial constraints.

## Rack Strategy

Two core strategies remain possible:

- **rack-first**: sample a rack, then search for a legal move that uses it;
- **witness-first**: construct a legal move, then derive a rack that contains its required new symbols.

Witness-first generation guarantees solvability more directly. Rack-first generation can provide more natural rack distributions but requires stronger search or certification.
